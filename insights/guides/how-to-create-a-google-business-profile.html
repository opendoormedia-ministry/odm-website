How to Create a Google Business Profile

Open Door Media · October 12, 2026

A Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) puts your organization on Google Search and Google Maps so people can find you, see your hours, read reviews, and contact you — for free. This guide walks you through creating and verifying your profile from scratch.

Part 1: Sign In and Start Your Profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and click Manage now.
  2. Sign in with a Google account you want associated with this business. If you don't have one, click Create account and follow the prompts.
    • Use a business-owned Google account if possible (e.g., one tied to your organization's Gmail), rather than a personal account.

Part 2: Enter Your Business Details

  1. Type your business name in the search field. If it appears in the dropdown, your profile may already exist — click it and follow the prompts to claim it. Otherwise, click Add your business to Google.
  2. Choose the business category that best describes what you do (e.g., "Nonprofit organization," "Photographer," "Church"). You can add more categories later.
  3. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?":
    • Select Yes if you have a physical storefront, office, or venue people come to.
    • Select No if you're service-area based (e.g., you travel to clients).
  4. If you selected Yes, enter your full street address.
  5. If you selected No (or in addition to a physical location), you'll be asked to define your service area — add the cities, counties, or regions you serve.
  6. Enter your business phone number and website URL (if you have one). If your site isn't live yet, you can leave the website field blank and add it later.
  7. Click Next to continue.

Part 3: Verify Your Business

Google requires verification before your profile goes live. The method offered to you depends on your business type and location. Common options include:

MethodHow It Works
PostcardGoogle mails a postcard with a 5-digit code to your business address. This usually takes 5–14 days. Enter the code at business.google.com when it arrives.
Phone / TextGoogle calls or texts a verification code to your business phone number. Enter it immediately.
EmailGoogle sends a verification link to the email on file. Click it to verify.
Video recordingFor some business types, Google may request a short video showing your location, signage, or equipment. Follow the on-screen instructions.
Live video callA Google representative will join a call to verify your business in real time. Schedule it when prompted.

Select the method available to you and follow the prompts. Your profile will remain in a limited "pending verification" state until the code is confirmed.

Tip: If you're waiting on a postcard, you can still fill out your profile in the meantime — add photos, hours, and a description — it just won't be publicly visible until verification is complete.

Part 4: Complete Your Profile

Once verified (or while you wait), fill in as much detail as possible. A complete profile ranks better and earns more trust.

  • Business hours — including any special holiday hours
  • Description — a brief summary of what you do and who you serve (750 characters max)
  • Photos — a logo, a cover photo, and images of your space, team, or work
  • Services or products — list what you offer
  • Attributes — things like "wheelchair accessible," "veteran-owned," "nonprofit," etc.

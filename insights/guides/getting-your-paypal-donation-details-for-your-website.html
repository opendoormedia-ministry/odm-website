Getting Your PayPal Donation Details for Your Website

Open Door Media · July 24, 2026

Legacy markdown guide. The stored content is in markdown format. Clear this notice and type/paste the content to re-enter it in the new editor, then save to convert it.

To set up online donations on your website, I need one small piece of information from your PayPal account. It's called a Client ID — think of it like a name tag PayPal uses to recognize your organization's account. It is not your password, and you will never need to share your password with me.

This should take about 10 minutes. If you get stuck at any point, stop and call me — I'm happy to walk through this together on the phone while you're looking at your screen.
Step 1: Go to the PayPal Developer website
Open your web browser (the app you use to look at websites — like Safari, Chrome, or Edge).

In the address bar at the top, type exactly this and press Enter:

developer.paypal.com

Look for a button in the top-right corner that says Log into Dashboard. Click it.
Step 2: Log in
Log in the same way you normally log into PayPal — using your usual PayPal email and password.

This is the only time you'll use your password. You're logging into PayPal's own site, not giving it to anyone.
Step 3: Find "My Apps & Credentials"
Once you're logged in, look near the top of the page for a link or tab that says Apps & Credentials. Click it.
Step 4: Create an App
Look for a button that says Create App.

It will ask for an App Name — you can type: `Website Donations`

Click Create App.
Step 5: Copy the Client ID
After the app is created, you'll see a page with some information on it.

Look for a box labeled Client ID. It will be a long string of letters and numbers.

There should be a small button next to it that says Copy — click that.

If there's no Copy button, you can click into the box, select all the text (drag your mouse across it or press Ctrl+A on a PC / Cmd+A on a Mac), and copy it that way.
Step 6: Send it to me
Send me that Client ID any way that's easiest for you:
- Text it to me
- Email it to me
- Or just read it out loud to me over the phone and I'll type it

Please do NOT send me:
- Your PayPal password
- Anything labeled "Secret" (if you happen to see that word on the page, skip it — I'll ask for that separately later if it's ever needed)
That's it!
Once I have the Client ID, I can handle everything else on my end. Thank you for bearing with the tech stuff — I really appreciate it, and I'm just a call away if anything looks confusing on screen.

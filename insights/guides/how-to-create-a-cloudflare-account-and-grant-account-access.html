How to Create a Cloudflare Account and Grant Account Access

Open Door Media · July 24, 2026

This guide walks you through creating a free Cloudflare account and adding your developer as a member so they can manage your site.

Part 1: Create Your Cloudflare Account

  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com
  2. Click Sign up.
  3. Enter:
    • Email address: YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS
    • Password: CREATE A SECURE PASSWORD
  4. Check your inbox for a verification link and click it to confirm your account.
  5. If prompted to select a plan, choose Free unless your developer says otherwise.
  6. Skip any setup prompts about adding a domain — your developer will handle that part.

You now have a Cloudflare account. Keep your login somewhere safe — a password manager is ideal.

Part 2: Add Your Developer as a Member with Admin Access

This gives your developer full access to manage your Cloudflare account — including Pages, DNS, and settings — while you stay the account owner. No need to create a project first; this happens at the account level.

  1. Log in and confirm the correct account is selected (top of the sidebar, if you have more than one).
  2. Click Manage Account in the left sidebar → select Members.
  3. Click Invite.
  4. Enter your developer's email: DEVELOPER'S EMAIL ADDRESS
  5. Under Roles, choose one:
RoleWhat it gives them
AdministratorFull access to manage Pages, DNS, and settings — billing and member management stay with you
Super AdministratorEverything above, plus billing and member management
Recommended: Administrator, unless you want your developer to have full unrestricted control.
  1. Click Continue to summary.
  2. Review the details, then click Invite.
Note: Your developer must accept the email invitation before their access is active.

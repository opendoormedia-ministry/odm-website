<p>This guide walks you through creating a free Cloudflare account and adding Open Door Media as a member so we can manage your site.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Part 1: Create Your Cloudflare Account</h2>
<ol><li>Go to <a href="https://dash.cloudflare.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">dash.cloudflare.com</a></li><li>Click <strong>Sign up</strong>.</li><li>Enter:<ul><li><strong>Email address:</strong> <code>YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS</code></li><li><strong>Password:</strong> <code>CREATE A SECURE PASSWORD</code></li></ul></li><li>Check your inbox for a verification link and click it to confirm your account.</li><li>If prompted to select a plan, choose <strong>Free</strong> unless we have told you otherwise.</li><li>Skip any setup prompts about adding a domain — we will handle that part.</li></ol>
<p>You now have a Cloudflare account. Keep your login somewhere safe — a password manager is ideal.</p>
<hr>
<h2>Part 2: Add Your Developer as a Member with Admin Access</h2>
<p>This gives us full access to manage your Cloudflare account — including Pages, DNS, and settings — while you stay the account owner. No need to create a project first; this happens at the account level.</p>
<ol><li>Log in and confirm the correct account is selected (top of the sidebar, if you have more than one).</li><li>Click <strong>Manage Account</strong> in the left sidebar → select <strong>Members</strong>.</li><li>Click <strong>Invite</strong>.</li><li>Enter our email: <strong>project@opendoormedia.us</strong></li><li>Under <strong>Roles</strong>, choose one:</li></ol>
<table><thead><tr><th>Role</th><th>What it gives them</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Administrator</strong></td><td>Full access to manage Pages, DNS, and settings — billing and member management stay with you</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Super Administrator</strong></td><td>Everything above, plus billing and member management</td></tr></tbody></table>
<blockquote><strong>Recommended:</strong> Administrator, unless we arrange to have billing access.</blockquote>
<ol><li>Click <strong>Continue to summary</strong>.</li><li>Review the details, then click <strong>Invite</strong>.</li></ol>
<blockquote><strong>Note:</strong> We must accept the email invitation before our access is active.</blockquote>

<p>To set up online donations on your website, we'll need one small piece of information from your PayPal account. It's called a <strong>Client ID </strong>— think of it like a name tag PayPal uses to recognize your organization's account. It is <strong>not </strong>your password, and you will never need to share your password with us.</p><hr><h3>Step 1: Go to the PayPal Developer website</h3><p>Open your web browser. In the address bar at the top, go to this URL:</p><p><a href="developer.paypal.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">developer.paypal.com</a></p><p>Look for a button in the top-right corner that says <strong>Log into Dashboard</strong>. Click it.</p><hr><h3>Step 2: Log in</h3><p>Log in the same way you normally log into PayPal — using your usual PayPal email and password.</p><blockquote>This is the only time you'll use your password. You're logging into PayPal's own site, not giving it to anyone.</blockquote><hr><h3>Step 3: Create an App</h3><p>Once you're logged in, look near the top of the page for a link or tab that says <strong>Apps &amp; Credentials</strong>. Click it.</p><p>Look for a button that says <strong>Create App</strong>.</p><p>It will ask for an App Name — you can type: "Website Donations"</p><p>Click <strong>Create App</strong>.</p><hr><h3>Step 4: Copy the Client ID</h3><p>After the app is created, you'll see a page with some information on it.</p><p>Look for a box labeled Client ID. It will be a long string of letters and numbers.</p><p>There should be a small button next to it that says Copy — click that.</p>

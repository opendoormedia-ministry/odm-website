To set up online donations on your website, I need one small piece of information from your PayPal account. It's called a **Client ID** — think of it like a name tag PayPal uses to recognize your organization's account. It is **not** your password, and you will never need to share your password with me.

This should take about 10 minutes. If you get stuck at any point, stop and call me — I'm happy to walk through this together on the phone while you're looking at your screen.

---

## Step 1: Go to the PayPal Developer website

1. Open your web browser (the app you use to look at websites — like Safari, Chrome, or Edge).
2. In the address bar at the top, type exactly this and press Enter:

   `developer.paypal.com`

3. Look for a button in the top-right corner that says **Log into Dashboard**. Click it.

---

## Step 2: Log in

1. Log in the same way you normally log into PayPal — using your usual PayPal email and password.

   > This is the only time you'll use your password. You're logging into PayPal's own site, not giving it to anyone.

---

## Step 3: Find "My Apps & Credentials"

1. Once you're logged in, look near the top of the page for a link or tab that says **Apps & Credentials**. Click it.

---

## Step 4: Create an App

1. Look for a button that says **Create App**.
2. It will ask for an App Name — you can type: `Website Donations`
3. Click **Create App**.

---

## Step 5: Copy the Client ID

1. After the app is created, you'll see a page with some information on it.
2. Look for a box labeled **Client ID**. It will be a long string of letters and numbers.
3. There should be a small button next to it that says **Copy** — click that.

   > If there's no Copy button, you can click into the box, select all the text (drag your mouse across it or press Ctrl+A on a PC / Cmd+A on a Mac), and copy it that way.

---

Send me that Client ID any way that's easiest for you:
